Milestone escrow

Money moves only when you approve.

Your money sits in escrow, not with the builder — funded one milestone at a time as each begins, never the whole job upfront. A milestone is released only after the builder's QC passes, the builder's site engineer signs off, and you approve last. Both of those sign-offs are the builder's own staff — you are the only independent check, and the money does not move until you say so. Try it.
